About Our School

Education That Inspires Excellence

Siqarah Public Model High School, Shorkot, is committed to supporting students on their educational journey. Our aim is to make learning meaningful, encourage curiosity and help students develop the skills they need for the future.

We value education, discipline, respect, personal responsibility and a positive relationship between students, teachers and parents. We want every student to feel encouraged to learn and improve.
